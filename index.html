Ask about our range
What products does Farm Fresh offer?+

Explore milk, plain, flavoured, Greek and natural yogurts, cheese, salted and unsalted butter, bulk butter formats and desi ghee. Each product lists its available packs.

How do I choose a pack size?+

Open a product, choose a listed pack and add it to your enquiry. If no weight is listed, ask our team about available sizes.

Can I place an order on this website?+

This site prepares enquiry previews; it does not send enquiries, take payments or confirm orders. Availability, pricing and delivery can be discussed once official contact channels are connected.

Do you work with shops and food businesses?+

Yes. Retailers, distributors, wholesalers, cafés, hotels and restaurants can prepare an enquiry with their business type, city, products and quantities.

Where can I find ingredients and storage details?+

Check your product's packaging for ingredients, allergens, expiry and storage instructions. Add any further questions to your enquiry.
